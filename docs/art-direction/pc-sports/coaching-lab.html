Roster · Who earns the role?

Team · Where does everyone fit?

Recruiting · What should I learn next?

Needs narrow the board. Scout estimates remain ranges; uncertainty and knowledge gaps drive the next action. Spend feedback stays with the player. Demo scouting costs are placeholders, not a new economy.
