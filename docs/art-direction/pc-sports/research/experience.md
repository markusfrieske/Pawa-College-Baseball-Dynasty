# PC experience research: entry, story and legacy

These 29 briefs replace the mobile-responsive assumptions of setup-stories.json. They are proposals, not implemented behavior. The approved Class of Nine forest, brass, cream and campus gateway remain; arrival cinematics remain unapproved.

## Critical conclusions

A full-screen website remains a website if every destination repeats a banner, four cards and a long scroll. Changing corner radius alone will not fix this. Each screen needs its own dominant decision, physical scale and information rhythm. Entry is a scene; correspondence is a queue and letter; rankings are a broadcast slate; history is a wall and year index; a correction is a paired evidence sheet. They should share typography, selection behavior and context, not the same template.

PC is the target. Design at 1920x1080 and verify 1280x720, 2560x1440 and 3440x1440. Keep essential controls within the viewport. Long datasets belong in a bounded, keyboard-accessible pane with page/count and deliberate position restoration. Do not hide overflow blindly. Prefer metric groups over horizontal overflow. Provide text scaling before filling ultrawide space with extra panels. Every visible command needs a real mouse target; no controller-only navigation or fake keyboard hints.

Do not add walkable offices, new economics, fabricated dialogue, licensed art or unsupported award systems to simulate production value. Use original campus art as the focal element only where it improves identity or emotion. Busy imagery behind data reduces quality.

## Three implementation priorities

1. Build the PC frame and interaction contract first: top context, destination tabs, clear selection, Escape/focus restoration, bounded panes and text scaling. Remove bottom mobile navigation and infinite body layouts. Test input rather than drawing unused key legends.
2. Implement three visibly different representatives before spreading styles: title/dynasty locker, correspondence, and a standings/statistical board. Compare silhouettes at thumbnail size; reject them if their layouts look interchangeable.
3. Build editorial and legacy screens from real stored evidence: weekly edition, honors stage, archive volumes and correction spread. Guard pending actions and retain selection on errors. Do not attach an unapproved arrival sequence to season recap.

The companion JSON contains every original screen ID, concrete zones, interaction, failure handling and acceptance criteria. These briefs require runtime and visual validation before being marked complete.
