| --- | --- | --- |

All four are shown at large size and actual 64, 48 and 32 pixels. Recommend B as primary with a later simplified derivative for small uses; D shows the useful alternate emphasis. Do not automatically adopt two unrelated marks as a system. A tiny icon may need further simplification; these are comparison specimens, not validated final app assets.

The SVGs use live Georgia bold italic or Arial bold text for C9; the wordmark stays the selected Georgia bold italic. Final outlined lettering, optical adjustments and commercial clearance remain separate. The previous approved gateway asset is preserved.
