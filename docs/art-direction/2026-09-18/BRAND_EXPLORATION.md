Frisk selected **Class of Nine + L04 campus gateway + L02 lettering** on September 18. [Focused combined preview](../2026-09-17/class-of-nine.html). The actual L02 preview uses `Georgia, serif`, italic, weight 800, with tight letter spacing. Preserve that appearance rather than the original L04 uppercase sans-serif. Name/symbol/lettering are selected; descriptor, tagline, final outlined assets and commercial clearance are not newly approved by this choice.

## Four logo styles

- **L01 Letterman plate:** athletic wordmark and initials in home plate. Recommended all-purpose identity; small icon and full wordmark variants. Final lettering should be custom-refined after selection.
- **L02 Championship pennant:** a diamond inside a pennant; expressive serif title. Warm celebration/recruit-class identity; generic flag symbolism requires distinctive execution.
- **L03 Living record book:** open scorebook and diamond; editorial serif. Connects text simulation, league tracking and history; risks looking like a stats tool.
- **L04 Campus gateway:** an original campus arch and home plate; spacious modern lettering. Connects miniature campus and arrival narrative; avoid resemblance to an actual university seal.

Retain forest, brass and cream, friendly UI typography and original cel players. Separate the application's identity from fictional team identities. Do not imitate Power Pros/PAWAPURO lettering, character designs or publisher logos. Existing game references or assets need a separate inventory before a commercial launch; a name change does not settle their rights.

## Preliminary search record and limits

Exploratory exact-phrase web searches were run September 18 for each shortlisted name. Results did not establish availability or legal clearance. No direct USPTO register search, jurisdictional assessment, domain/handle availability check or similarity search has been completed. Search-engine queries restricted to Steam for some early candidates returned off-domain/noisy results and are not Steam catalog clearance.

Candidates screened out or deprioritized:

- **Pennant & Pine:** existing sports merchandise brand surfaced on its [Faire storefront](https://www.faire.com/en-ca/brand/b_wwqeftfsvf); excluded from shortlist.
- **Letter Nine:** existing musical act on [Bandsintown](https://www.bandsintown.com/a/13587909-letter-nine); excluded as a naming/search-discovery judgment, not an infringement determination.
- **Clubhouse Years:** existing podcast title usage and weak product specificity; not shortlisted.
- **Diamond Almanac:** more archival than game-like; search results also highlighted the crowded baseball reference category. Not shortlisted; no legal determination.
- **Pennant Grove:** geographic usage surfaced. Retained as a creative option only; unrelated geographic results do not prove trademark availability.

U.S. naming concerns are primarily trademark concerns: the [Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html) explains that names/titles are not copyright-protected, while original logo artwork may be. The [USPTO](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion) evaluates similarity alongside related goods/services. Use its [comprehensive clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) when a finalist is chosen. Have the finalist and logo cleared for intended launch markets before commercial commitment; these proposals do not guarantee non-infringement.
