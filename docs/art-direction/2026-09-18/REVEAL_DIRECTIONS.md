# PAWA player arrival: cinematic direction choices

Prepared for Frisk, September 18, 2026. **Creative options for selection; no reveal direction approved yet.** Frisk has authorized the selected Varsity Club visual system across the game. This new arrival ceremony is a separate creative decision within that system.

## Creative verdict

Make joining feel like a player finding a home in the program. The emotional payoff is a recognizable athlete wearing your colors, a useful reason to care about them, and a permanent memory you can revisit. A longer glow effect around a rating will not deliver that.

Recommend **Clubhouse Arrival**: glimpse the miniature campus at dusk, enter a warm locker vignette, reveal the player's nameplate, and resolve to a confident cel portrait in a clean sports profile. One short ceremony, then useful baseball information. Every arrival gets the same dignity; a low-rated developmental player can become the defining story of a dynasty.

Four complete directions are available below. The decision tables provide **40 choices across 10 facets**. Take the complete A, B, C or D recipe, or mix facets. All directions retain forest/brass surfaces, Sora/Inter, athletic monograms, original cel athletes and clear broadcast typography. These are treatments to compare, not four different game brands.

## Four complete directions

| Direction | Sequence and emotional promise | Best use | Main tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Clubhouse Arrival** | Campus at dusk → locker plaque → athlete in team uniform → sports profile. “You belong here.” | Default individual arrival; freshmen, transfers and walk-ons. | Needs a composed locker scene and consistent uniform kit. Most achievable premium direction. |
| **B — Signing Night Live** | On-air slate → monogram wipe → cel athlete and presenter-style lower-third → broadcast feature card. “The league should notice.” | Online league announcements and creator broadcasts. | Can feel impersonal if every player receives sensational commentary. Keep copy factual. |
| **C — The Next Chapter** | Program memory wall → new portrait placed beside season marker → short factual story → archival profile. “This is where their story continues.” | Dynasty storytelling, transfers and season retrospectives. | Warmest story direction, but slower and less immediately spectacular. Never invent quotes or backstory. |
| **D — First Step on the Diamond** | Miniature ballpark → short approach along the foul line → cel athlete under field lights → poster card. “Time to earn your place.” | Featured arrivals and optional season ceremonies. | Highest environment and animation cost; must read as a stylized vignette, not promise a playable 3D campus. |

## R01 — Reveal story and trigger

| Code | Choice | Payoff and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R01A** | **Clubhouse Arrival:** nameplate and jersey welcome the athlete. | Personal attachment without manufactured suspense. Use “Joined the roster” only after the authoritative roster transaction succeeds. |
| **R01B** | **Signing Night Live:** a verified league announcement introduces the player. | Strong companion utility; show league, season and exact event state. “Committed to” remains distinct from roster membership. |
| **R01C** | **The Next Chapter:** one true career fact connects the player to this season. | Transfers carry their existing history and identity. Fall back to position and entry type when no verified history exists. |
| **R01D** | **First Step on the Diamond:** the athlete enters the home ballpark. | Aspirational arrival. Never imply a guaranteed starting role, scholarship or eligibility outcome the game has not established. |

The event decides the wording, regardless of selected story. Interest changes are recruiting updates; commitments are commitments; confirmed roster additions are arrivals. Do not reuse “Signed,” “Committed” and “Joined” interchangeably. Power Pros companion announcements must distinguish an app roster change from any separate in-game roster recreation or commissioner approval.

## R02 — Staging and world art

| Code | Choice | Payoff and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R02A** | **Warm locker vignette:** miniature campus establishing image, forest locker, brass nameplate, folded uniform. | Strong selected-art continuity; two reusable scene plates and a uniform overlay. Frame the name away from busy art. |
| **R02B** | **Broadcast studio:** geometric field diagram, athletic monogram wall, warm key light. | Very scalable across teams; least scene-art dependency. Avoid generic neon sports graphics. |
| **R02C** | **Program memory wall:** restrained pinboard with season cards, campus photograph and arrival portrait. | Good dynasty continuity; previous seasons appear only when history exists. No decorative fabricated trophies. |
| **R02D** | **Miniature home diamond:** isometric ballpark with a lit route toward the dugout. | Best expression of the campus world; position the cel hero on a foreground plane sharing the same warm light. No live 3D requirement. |

## R03 — Animation language

| Code | Choice | Payoff and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R03A** | **Nameplate slide:** short campus dissolve, plaque slides into place, uniform-color wipe reveals player. | Approximately 4 seconds to final state. Calm anticipation with a clear final beat. |
| **R03B** | **Broadcast cut:** three deliberate cuts, monogram wipe, lower-third snap into place. | Approximately 3 seconds. Strong streaming rhythm; no camera shake or white flash. |
| **R03C** | **Memory placement:** portrait settles onto the wall, season title appears, camera gently reframes. | Approximately 4.5 seconds. Tactile warmth; use transform/opacity layers rather than simulated physics. |
| **R03D** | **Ballpark approach:** three-plane parallax toward dugout, lights warm, player steps into framing. | Approximately 6 seconds. Most cinematic and expensive; full-body walk cycle is optional future scope, not required for initial prototype. |

Times are proposed choreography targets, not measured runtime. The final information remains available immediately through Skip. Never lengthen the sequence because a hidden rating is higher. No continuous confetti, fireworks, looping light sweeps or surprise full-screen effects after the result is already visible.

## R04 — Final card and information hierarchy

| Code | Choice | Payoff and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R04A** | **Arrival sports profile:** large stable portrait; name, position, entry type and team; three authorized baseball details; roster action. | Best match to selected sports-profile direction. Full ratings stay in the expandable scouting/profile area. |
| **R04B** | **Broadcast feature card:** landscape portrait with strong name ribbon, program monogram and two stat tiles. | Readable on streams and shared landscape graphics. Less room for background and eligibility. |
| **R04C** | **Career chapter card:** portrait, season/event date, former program when applicable and one factual career line. | Best long-term memory; avoids reducing athletes to overall rating. Requires trustworthy history. |
| **R04D** | **Athletic poster:** full-height or waist-up hero, oversized name, position and team mark; details on a companion panel. | Highest visual impact and portrait-export appeal. Essential information must remain on the front; detail access cannot rely on hover or a 3D flip. |

Do not label frames with rarity, financial value or collectible scarcity. No packs, odds, rerolls or paid reveal language. A poster is a record of an athlete, not a randomized purchase. Show uncertain scout estimates as estimates; do not reveal latent potential through card color, ordering, effects, sound or badges.

## R05 — Player portrayal

| Code | Choice | Payoff and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R05A** | **Signature cel bust:** expressive face and shoulders, cap and current uniform, one restrained confident pose. | Most scalable and recognizable. Reuse stable appearance attributes from recruiting through archive. |
| **R05B** | **Media-day half-body:** athlete faces camera holding glove or bat with program backdrop. | Broadcast personality; equipment must match the known role and not obscure the face. |
| **R05C** | **Then-and-now portrait pair:** prior uniform and current uniform side by side for verified transfers; solo portrait for other arrivals. | Gives transfers narrative weight without changing their face, skin tone, hair or body identity. |
| **R05D** | **Position vignette:** waist-up pitcher set, catcher with mask raised, infielder glove-ready or hitter at rest. | Strong baseball identity; highest pose/gear art burden. Provide neutral fallback for utility players. |

Use original adult-athlete proportions, not franchise lookalikes. Distinguish players by facial structure, hair and silhouette as well as complexion. Emotional expression must not imply hidden personality mechanics. Jersey numbers are shown only when assigned; never invent a number for visual balance.

## R06 — Typography and copy

| Code | Choice | Payoff and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R06A** | **Warm welcome:** “Welcome to [Program]” above the name; event status and role below. | Human and restrained. Sora headline, Inter details; the event status remains factual. |
| **R06B** | **On-air headline:** “[Name] joins [Program]” or “[Name] commits to [Program],” according to event. | Most legible announcement and easiest to share. No “breaking” label on a replay. |
| **R06C** | **Chapter title:** “A new chapter in [Program]” followed by “Transfer from [Previous program]” when verified. | Warm dynasty storytelling. No generated first-person quotes or invented coach promises. |
| **R06D** | **Name-first poster:** player's name is the lead; “Meet your newest [team nickname]” only when roster addition is confirmed. | Strong visual identity. Use team name when no approved nickname exists. |

Support long names, accents and multiword teams. Do not force tiny text to fit a single line. Ratings use tabular numerals with their system identified; PAWA overall or scouting grades must not masquerade as Power Pros ratings.

## R07 — Sound treatment

| Code | Choice | Payoff and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R07A** | **Clubhouse signature:** quiet room tone, plaque click, soft glove pat and warm two-note resolve. | Closest to selected Clubhouse sound; intimate and comfortable through a class of players. |
| **R07B** | **Broadcast bumper:** short brushed-percussion pickup and one restrained brass chord. | Clean creator-friendly rhythm; one sting per sequence, no announcer required. |
| **R07C** | **Paper and room:** card placement, pencil stroke and subtle room ambience. | Warmest and least intrusive; relies on art and typography for impact. |
| **R07D** | **Ballpark evening:** faint outdoor ambience, cleat step and soft crowd rise at the final frame. | Strongest sense of place; avoid implying an actual crowd event or stadium attendance. |

Sound remains opt-in with persistent mute and independent music/effects levels. Every meaning has a visual equivalent. These are direction descriptions, **not auditioned samples**. Use original or appropriately licensed audio; mute music in exported clips by default and provide a silent export. No autoplay before user interaction.

## R08 — Sharing and keepsakes

| Code | Choice | Payoff and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R08A** | **Arrival announcement kit:** preview/download 4:5 portrait and 16:9 landscape PNGs, plus editable plain-text caption. | Most useful first release: reliable assets for Discord, social posts and league recap. Manual sharing only. |
| **R08B** | **Broadcast replay clip:** 6–8 second export with a readable held final frame, silent by default. | Best motion showcase; encoding, platform support and capture fidelity require a separate implementation gate. |
| **R08C** | **Program scrapbook entry:** permanent in-game event page plus downloadable chapter card. | Best dynasty memory. External link sharing depends on actual visibility/auth rules; private league URLs stay private. |
| **R08D** | **Creator overlay pack:** transparent player/name graphic and flat background panel for streams. | Most flexible creator tool; highest crop/alpha/export QA cost. Do not include private scouting information. |

The user reviews the exact public payload before download or opening a native share dialog. Default exports include name, team, position, season and accurate event label. Scouting detail is excluded unless explicitly selected and authorized for that audience. Never export hidden ratings, private recruiting offers, contact details or real-world personal data merely because the screen can see them. Replays preserve the event's historical team/season instead of silently using the player's latest transfer destination.

## R09 — Full-class presentation

| Code | Choice | Payoff and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R09A** | **Choose the spotlight:** class summary appears first; watch any individual arrival and return to the same position in the grid. | Best everyday experience and low repetition; user decides who matters. Class index includes clear viewed/unviewed state. |
| **R09B** | **Signing-night rundown:** short class intro, user-controlled next/previous arrivals, final class sheet. | Best league-show pacing. Stop/resume between players; no forced autoplay marathon. |
| **R09C** | **Yearbook wall:** all portraits settle into stable slots, then select an athlete for their chapter. | Strong sense of collective identity. Alphabetical or user-selected sorting; do not spoil concealed information through order. |
| **R09D** | **Position-unit roll call:** introduce pitchers, catchers, infield and outfield groups, then complete team poster. | Connects arrivals to roster needs. Utility/two-way players keep clear roles without duplicate roster counts. |

Zero arrivals receives an honest empty state, not a failed ceremony. Large classes remain searchable and keyboard navigable. Replaying a sequence never re-adds a player, advances a season, consumes a resource or changes scouting knowledge.

## R10 — Pacing and accessible presentation

| Code | Choice | Payoff and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R10A** | **Short ceremony:** recommended 4-second sequence, Skip always visible, Replay and View player on the final state. | Default emotional beat with minimal delay. Reduced motion opens the completed profile immediately. |
| **R10B** | **Coach's cut:** 1.5-second identity transition, then full card. | Best frequent companion use and long classes. Reduced motion remains instantaneous. |
| **R10C** | **Step-through story:** user advances between 3 static illustrated panels; final card is always directly accessible. | Gives control over reading and storytelling; slightly more interaction cost. Keyboard and touch have equal paths. |
| **R10D** | **Optional premiere:** user explicitly chooses an 8-second expanded sequence, with pause/skip and a static equivalent. | Best special-session showcase; never the mandatory route to roster information. |

Accessibility is a baseline for every option, not a choice to opt out of. Preserve focus, visible controls, accessible status text and Escape/close behavior. Never hide keyboard-focusable controls behind the inactive card face. Respect reduced-motion preference changes while open; stop parallax, flips, particles, flashes and automatic pans. Skipping must settle all effects and audio immediately and reach the identical truthful final state.

## Recommended first production recipe

**R01A · R02A · R03A · R04A · R05A · R06A · R07A · R08A · R09A · R10A.**

This is a recommendation for Frisk to review, not an approved selection. It pairs a warm arrival ceremony with an efficient class index and usable announcement exports. Keep expert density in the surrounding roster and recruiting screens; the optional four-second ceremony earns one expressive moment without making daily management sparse.

Proposed timing: **0.0–0.6 s** campus establish → **0.6–1.4 s** locker/nameplate → **1.4–2.3 s** player and uniform reveal → **2.3–3.2 s** name/status/role → **3.2–4.0 s** settle to profile and actions. Avoid revealing only shoes, flags or rating colors as arbitrary suspense clues. The player's identity and their actual place in the program are the reveal.

First asset scope: one miniature campus plate, one locker plate, one modular cel bust kit, team monogram/uniform overlays, one motion timeline, one optional sound sting, and two static export layouts. Prototype timing with existing art; final character consistency and uniform coverage need an art acceptance pass before claiming production completeness.

## Review gates before rollout

1. **Truth and recovery:** verify interest, commitment, confirmed arrival, transfer and replay states. Disconnect during completion/acknowledgment; retry cannot duplicate players or change the event. Display server failure without a false success ceremony.
2. **Art and identity:** review at least 24 varied cel athletes across four team palettes; same face at recruiting, reveal, roster and transfer. Check cap/skin/hair contrast at 40, 80 and 200 px. Record gaps in the modular kit rather than substituting unrelated portraits.
3. **Interaction:** desktop and 320/390 px phone; long names, absent portrait, zero and large classes, keyboard-only, reduced motion toggled mid-sequence, persistent mute, immediate skip and replay. No essential action depends on waiting through animation.
4. **Exports:** both aspect ratios, maximum names, private/public data boundaries, old-season replay and transferred players. Download a real file and inspect it; a working preview alone is not export evidence.
5. **Game-dev review:** independently ask whether the player has a clear identity, understands what actually changed, can find the next useful baseball action, and still wants to watch the fifth arrival. Source correctness, runtime proof and human enjoyment are separate acceptance records.

The decision requested from Frisk is the preferred direction and any mixed facet codes. Production integration of the already selected game-wide UI can proceed independently while this ceremony remains under creative review.
