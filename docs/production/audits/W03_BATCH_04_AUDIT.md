## Transaction and retention findings

The [finalizer](../../../server/game-finalizer.ts) takes league KEY SHARE, game FOR UPDATE, the per-league projection advisory lock, then sorted coach row locks before recording coach projections. Shared league locks allow independent finalizers to begin while preventing restore from obtaining its destructive league lock. Restore never waits for an advisory lock while holding games, so the reviewed participating paths introduce no circular lock dependency. Game identity and prepared schedule fields are rechecked; an already completed game without a matching receipt requires reconciliation rather than fresh effects.

The [migration](../../../server/migrations/0053_game_coach_effects.sql) creates a unique game/coach contribution tied to its finalization receipt. Existing receipts have no invented contributions. Coach IDs remain historical identifiers rather than foreign keys: deleting or replacing a coach does not silently remove prior attribution. Receipt/league deletion can intentionally cascade owned effects. The rows are append-only through the reviewed application paths, **not database-immutable**: there is no effect-update/delete trigger. This is a durable coaching contribution ledger, not a complete player/stat/rest reversal ledger.

Actual award observations include XP, levels, skill points, career/conference records, legacy score and perks. They document the values committed by this game. They do not reconstruct omitted older awards or establish stable historical rules/roster/authority manifests. Suppressed coach effects and exhibition games intentionally create no coach contribution rows; an old receipt without effects must not be interpreted as zero historical contribution.

Required coaching, effect and rivalry writes occur before acceptance revision/audit commit. Any required failure must roll back the entire official-result transaction. Cache invalidation and recap generation remain post-commit ancillary work. A supplied accumulator is not populated by atomic finalization; an empty later flush is a no-op. Exported legacy `finalizeGame` and `batchFinalizeGames` retain their older contract; source search finds no current production invocation, only the direct batch regression call. Their unused simulation imports are removed. This audit does not certify those legacy helpers.

## Retained release gates

- Postseason championship/appearance awards in `awardPostseasonXp`, achievement records, other coach/perk purchase writers and legacy helpers do not share the new game receipt identity. They require durable independent event identity and common update coordination; TI-09 stays implementing.
- The advisory lock serializes participating finalizers only. It does not make unrelated player/coaching/standings writers safe or order older reported games chronologically. Pitcher rest and rivalry last/streak fields still reflect completion order. Same-day doubleheader batches simulate before preceding batches commit, permitting stale rest inputs. W05 chronological scheduling remains open.
- Fail-closed source containment must receive real advance failure/restart/lease-loss tests. Checkpoint correctness, standard postseason helpers, durable notifications/news and whole-stage exactly-once behavior remain open under TI-08/W05.
- Restore is temporarily unavailable for leagues with reports or played results. Save capture is not complete recovery evidence. Consistent full inventory capture, compatibility and mature-league restoration remain W04 requirements.
- Coach effects alone cannot support accepted-result amendments, aggregate reversal/rebuild, immutable evidence or a full simulated-result identity contract. Those W03 obligations remain open.
- Browser styling, full authenticated page flows, complete-media builds, performance at season scale and player enjoyment were not tested by this backend slice. No edition-specific Power Pros rule was invented.
